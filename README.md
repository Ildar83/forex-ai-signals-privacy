# Forex AI Signals Privacy Policy

This public repository hosts the privacy policy for the Android application **Forex AI Signals** (`com.app.forexaisignals`).

The published GitHub Pages URL is intended for the Google Play Console privacy-policy field and for an in-app privacy link.

## Important Maintenance Note

The policy must be reviewed whenever the app’s advertising SDKs, permissions, account features, analytics, data collection, or third-party integrations change. The Google Play Data safety form should remain consistent with the policy and the actual app behavior.

This is a working policy document and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before it is relied upon for regulatory compliance.
