# Privacy Policy for Forex AI Signals

**Effective date:** August 28, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Forex AI Signals** (the “App”), published by **Ildar83** (the “Developer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App. The App provides informational Forex-related content, displays advertising, and allows users to open the public Telegram channel **@AITrade_Signals**.

## 1. Information the App Does Not Request Directly

The App does not require registration or creation of a user account. We do not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment information, contacts, messages, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, precise location, or financial account information.

The App does not execute trades, process payments, or connect to brokerage accounts.

## 2. Advertising and Automatically Processed Information

The App uses the **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK** to display a banner advertisement and an interstitial advertisement. The interstitial advertisement may be shown when a user chooses to open the Telegram channel.

The Yandex Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process information needed to request, deliver, secure, personalize where permitted, and measure advertising. Depending on device settings, permissions, jurisdiction, and Yandex configuration, this may include:

- IP address and general network information;
- advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or other device-related identifiers when available and permitted;
- device model, operating-system version, language, and other technical device parameters;
- mobile country code, mobile network code, connection type, and similar network parameters;
- advertisement requests, impressions, clicks, dismissals, and related advertising interactions;
- information used for fraud prevention, security, compliance, and advertising measurement.

Yandex states that its Mobile Ads SDK transfers relevant data off the device for advertising and that listed SDK data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.[1] Yandex’s own advertising privacy policy describes additional details about its processing, legal bases, recipients, retention, international transfers, and user rights.[2]

We do not receive a list containing your name or direct contact details from the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. We do not sell personal or sensitive user data.

## 3. Purpose of Processing

Information processed through the advertising SDK may be used to:

- display banner and interstitial advertisements;
- select and deliver advertisements, including personalized advertising where permitted;
- measure advertisement delivery and performance;
- detect invalid traffic, abuse, and fraud;
- maintain security and comply with legal obligations;
- improve advertising services.

Where applicable law requires consent for personalized advertising or similar processing, the relevant consent and advertising choices should be provided before such processing begins. You can also review or reset your Android advertising identifier and manage supported advertising choices in your device settings.

## 4. Telegram and External Links

When you tap a Telegram button, the App may first display a Yandex interstitial advertisement. After the advertisement is closed—or immediately if the advertisement is unavailable—the App asks Android to open the Telegram channel URL. The App itself does not send your name, contact details, or message content to Telegram.

Once Telegram or a web browser opens, your interaction is governed by the privacy policy and terms of that external service. Telegram’s privacy policy is available on its official website.[3]

## 5. Sharing of Information

The App may facilitate processing or sharing of limited device and advertising information with **Yandex** through the embedded Mobile Ads SDK for the purposes described above. External applications such as Telegram receive information according to their own systems only after you choose to open them.

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or to protect users, the App, or the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats. We do not rent or sell personal information.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

The Developer does not operate an App user-account database and does not retain user profiles, trading records, brokerage credentials, or payment information through the App.

Information processed by Yandex is retained according to Yandex’s policies and applicable law. Requests concerning advertising data processed by Yandex may be submitted through the contact methods provided in Yandex’s privacy policy.[2]

For privacy questions or deletion requests directed to the Developer, use the contact mechanism in Section 12. If we hold information that can reasonably be linked to your request, we will evaluate and respond as required by applicable law. Because the App does not create user accounts, there is no App account to delete.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures intended to limit unnecessary data access and keep the App’s data handling proportionate to its functions. The App does not request sensitive storage, overlay, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or financial-account permissions. Yandex states that data listed in its Mobile Ads SDK disclosure is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.[1]

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of third-party systems.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a channel controlled by the Developer, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Yandex describes possible international transfers and the safeguards it applies in its advertising privacy policy.[2]

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal, or portability of personal information. To exercise rights relating to data controlled by Yandex, contact Yandex using the methods in its privacy policy.[2] For requests relating to the Developer, use the contact mechanism below.

You may stop future App-related processing by uninstalling the App. You may manage advertising identifiers and supported ad-personalization settings through Android settings. These choices do not necessarily remove information already lawfully retained by a third party.
