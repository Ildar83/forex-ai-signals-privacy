Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Forex AI Signals (the “App”), published by Ildar83 (the “Developer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). The App provides informational Forex-related content, displays advertising, and lets users open the public Telegram channel @AITrade_Signals.

1. Information the App Does Not Request Directly

The App does not require registration or creation of a user account. We do not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment information, contacts, messages, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, precise location, or financial account information.

The App does not execute trades, process payments, or connect to brokerage accounts.

2. Advertising and Automatically Processed Information

The App uses the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK to display a banner advertisement and an interstitial advertisement. The interstitial advertisement may be shown when a user chooses to open the Telegram channel.

Depending on device settings, permissions, jurisdiction, and Yandex configuration, the SDK may process IP and network information; advertising or device identifiers; device model, operating-system version, language, and technical parameters; mobile country and network codes; advertisement requests and interactions; and information used for advertising measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Yandex states that relevant Mobile Ads SDK data is transferred off the device and encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. See Yandex’s Mobile Ads data-safety disclosure and Yandex Ads Privacy Policy.

We do not receive a list containing your name or direct contact details from the SDK, and we do not sell personal or sensitive user data.

3. Purpose of Processing

Advertising-related information may be used to:

  • display banner and interstitial advertisements;
  • select and deliver ads, including personalized ads where permitted;
  • measure advertisement delivery and performance;
  • detect invalid traffic, abuse, and fraud;
  • maintain security, comply with law, and improve advertising services.

Where applicable law requires consent, the relevant consent and advertising choices should be provided before such processing begins. Android advertising identifiers and supported personalization choices can also be managed through device settings.

4. Telegram and External Links

When you tap a Telegram button, the App may first display a Yandex interstitial ad. After the ad is closed—or immediately if it is unavailable—the App asks Android to open the Telegram channel URL. The App itself does not send your name, contact details, or message content to Telegram.

Once Telegram or a browser opens, the external service’s own privacy terms apply. See the Telegram Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of Information

Limited device and advertising information may be processed or shared with Yandex through the embedded Mobile Ads SDK for the purposes described above. External apps such as Telegram receive information according to their own systems after you choose to open them. We do not rent or sell personal information.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or to protect users, the App, or the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The Developer does not operate an App user-account database and does not retain user profiles, trading records, brokerage credentials, or payment information through the App. Because the App does not create user accounts, there is no App account to delete.

Information processed by Yandex is retained under Yandex’s policies and applicable law. Requests concerning advertising data processed by Yandex can be submitted through the contact methods in the Yandex Ads Privacy Policy.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures intended to limit unnecessary data access and keep data handling proportionate to the App’s functions. The App does not request sensitive storage, overlay, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or financial-account permissions. Yandex states that data listed in its Mobile Ads SDK disclosure is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security of third-party systems cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information through a channel controlled by the Developer.

9. International Data Transfers

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Yandex describes possible international transfers and safeguards in its advertising privacy policy.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal, or portability. Contact Yandex for data it controls and use the Developer contact below for Developer-related requests.

You may stop future App-related processing by uninstalling the App and may manage advertising identifiers and supported ad-personalization settings through Android.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s features, providers, or legal obligations change. The current version will remain available at this URL, and the “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.
